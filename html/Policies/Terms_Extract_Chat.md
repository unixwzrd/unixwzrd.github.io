## What the app does

Extract Chat is a local file-conversion utility from Distributed Thinking Systems LLC. It converts supported conversation JSON and ZIP archives into Markdown, HTML, and optional continuity bundles. It is an independent utility, not ChatGPT, and is not affiliated with or endorsed by OpenAI.

You choose which files to convert and where to save the results. Only process and share material you are entitled to use. You retain your rights in your conversations and files; using Extract Chat does not transfer them to us.

## Conversion limits and your originals

Conversation formats can change. A conversion may not include information that is missing, unsupported, unavailable, or absent from the source archive. Extract Chat does not recover deleted conversations, missing attachments, or private source objects from shared links. Converted documents and continuity chunks are derived outputs, not a certification of authorship, completeness, or provenance.

Keep your original archives and review important results before relying on or sharing them. The app does not modify input archives. An explicit overwrite choice can replace a previous managed result, so keep any result you want to preserve separately.
