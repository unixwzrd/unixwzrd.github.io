Adding a project to this site used to mean remembering every place it needed to appear. A YAML catalog now feeds generated metadata; Liquid places projects into the menu and Projects page; scaffolding gives a new project a landing page and its own update blog.

Part 5 follows that path, including the limits. Visibility fields change presentation, not access control, and a generator's starter article still needs an editor. I also explain how the Blog sections and front matter give technical posts, series, tutorials, and project updates different homes.
