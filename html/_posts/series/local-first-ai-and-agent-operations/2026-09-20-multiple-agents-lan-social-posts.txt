A second macOS account and a spare machine looked like a clean way to test another agent. Then I started asking what, exactly, the word "separate" was supposed to prove.

Part 13 works through the boundaries that a username does not settle: configuration authority, process ownership, ports, credentials, writable knowledge, shared inference, failure containment, and teardown.
