Body:

The main Part 9 article separates the speech bridge from the inference engine behind it. This Hands-On companion turns that boundary into something you can run without a model, GPU, provider account, or real voice.

The lab generates a short tone and invented transcript, starts a teaching bridge and fake OpenAI-compatible upstream on loopback, and runs 23 bounded acceptance checks. It verifies opaque-ID forwarding, bounded metadata-cache reuse, one legacy pair, capability-driven validation, explicit OGG-to-WAV fallback, independent health, redaction, and complete cleanup.

The package uses only the Python standard library and includes seven regressions. It is a protocol and failure-behavior lab, not a voice-quality demonstration.
