Use a small standard-library Python lab to reject incompatible or incorrect runs before calculating performance deltas.

Body:

Most benchmark examples begin with arithmetic. This one begins by asking whether the baseline and candidate still describe the same experiment.

Hands-On 10A packages that decision into eight inspectable files. The comparator validates a closed schema, checks artifact and workload identity, separates repeatability from optimization, rejects incompatible execution state, stops after correctness drift, and calculates median direction-aware deltas only for eligible runs.

Everything is synthetic. The lab requires no model, GPU, provider account, network service, or private evidence. The complete package, collapsed source viewers, 20-condition walkthrough, and nine regression tests are included.
