An exposed credential can become a business expense.

A leaked key can open a route to a paid cloud account, a customer system or a build pipeline. The response can mean investigating access, replacing credentials, reconfiguring applications and restoring interrupted work—before counting fraud or customer impact. Keeping fewer unnecessary copies and limiting which applications receive a credential helps reduce that exposure.
