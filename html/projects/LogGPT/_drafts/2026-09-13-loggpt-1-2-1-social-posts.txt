Large exports should not leave you wondering whether anything is happening.

LogGPT 1.2.1 is a usability-focused follow-up to the artifact preservation added in LogGPT Plus 1.2.0. Before an export begins, LogGPT Plus now shows total, generated, and uploaded artifact counts. During the export, it reports:

- the current phase
- the number of artifacts collected
- the filename being processed
- progress across the complete artifact set
- unavailable artifact counts
- ZIP creation progress

Long exports can also be cancelled during conversation retrieval, artifact collection, or ZIP creation. The browser download begins only after the archive is complete, so cancelling does not leave a partial ZIP behind.

The resulting archive continues to work directly with Extract Chat, which can create Markdown and HTML with references to the preserved local files.

Version 1.2.1 has been submitted to Apple and is currently in App Store review. The current LogGPT release remains available now; existing owners will receive the update at no additional charge once Apple approves it. LogGPT also supports Family Sharing.

Title:
LogGPT 1.2.1 Makes Large Artifact Exports Easier to Follow and Cancel

Subtitle:
See what LogGPT Plus found, follow each file as it is collected, and safely stop an export that is taking too long.

Body:

LogGPT Plus 1.2.0 added the ability to preserve a ChatGPT conversation and its generated or uploaded artifacts in one ZIP archive. Version 1.2.1 builds on that foundation with clearer feedback for conversations containing many files.

Before an export begins, the selection dialog now reports total, generated, and uploaded artifact counts. During collection, LogGPT displays the number completed, the current filename, a progress bar, and unavailable artifact information. ZIP creation also reports progress.

Long-running exports can now be cancelled during conversation retrieval, artifact collection, or ZIP creation. LogGPT creates the browser download only after the complete archive is ready, so cancelling an unfinished operation does not leave a partial ZIP behind. It also prevents duplicate exports while one is already being prepared.

Completed LogGPT Plus archives remain directly compatible with Extract Chat. The local utility can unpack the ZIP, organize its artifacts, and create Markdown, HTML, or both with references to the preserved files.

LogGPT 1.2.1 has been submitted to Apple and is currently in App Store review. The current release remains available from the same listing. Existing owners will receive version 1.2.1 at no additional charge after approval, and LogGPT supports Family Sharing.
