Title:
LogGPT 1.3.3 fixes missing export checkboxes after ChatGPT style changes - submitted to Apple

Body:
Disclosure: I develop LogGPT, a Safari extension for exporting ChatGPT conversations.

ChatGPT's page styles changed, and some of those styles were interfering with LogGPT's interface. Version 1.3.3 fixes missing checkboxes in the LogGPT Plus export dialog and Basic upgrade notice so the download options remain visible and usable.

For context, LogGPT exports conversation JSON. The optional Plus upgrade adds available generated and uploaded files in a ZIP with the conversation and an artifact manifest.

The update is in Apple's App Store review, not available yet. Existing owners will receive it at no additional charge once Apple approves and releases it.

Release notes: https://unixwzrd.ai/s/4cc849090f/
Project overview: https://unixwzrd.ai/projects/LogGPT/

If you've run into the missing-checkbox issue, this is the update addressing it.
