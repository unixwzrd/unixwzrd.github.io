A ChatGPT conversation is often more than text.

It may include generated images, documents, spreadsheets, audio, code archives, and files you uploaded to provide context. Exporting only the conversation JSON preserves the discussion, but it can leave an important part of the record behind.

LogGPT 1.2.0 is now available, along with the new LogGPT Plus upgrade for Safari.

The standard LogGPT workflow remains simple and unchanged: click the export button and download the conversation as structured JSON.

LogGPT Plus adds artifact preservation. You can choose generated content, uploaded content, or both, then download one ZIP containing:

- the conversation JSON
- the selected artifacts
- an artifact manifest with file identities, media types, sizes, hashes, source information, and download status

If an individual artifact cannot be downloaded, the rest of the archive is still saved and the failure is recorded in the manifest. The result is a useful capture package with an auditable account of what was preserved.

LogGPT has no user accounts, analytics, telemetry, or external processing service. The permanent Plus upgrade is available through the same Mac App Store listing.

Title:
LogGPT 1.2.0 and LogGPT Plus: Preserve Conversations and Artifacts

Subtitle:
ChatGPT conversations are more than text. LogGPT Plus can now preserve the files around them, too.

Body:

A useful ChatGPT conversation often contains more than a sequence of messages.

It may include generated images, documents, spreadsheets, audio, video, code archives, and files uploaded to provide context. The conversation JSON preserves the discussion and its structure, but those artifacts are also part of the record.

LogGPT 1.2.0 keeps the original export workflow intact. Click the export button and standard LogGPT downloads the conversation as structured JSON, just as before.

The new LogGPT Plus upgrade adds the option to preserve generated content, uploaded content, or both. It packages the conversation JSON and selected artifacts together in one ZIP archive, along with an artifact manifest describing what was found and downloaded.

The manifest records details such as stable file identifiers, media types, sizes, hashes, source information, and download status. Generated, uploaded, and derived files remain organized separately, and the original bytes are retained instead of being converted into another format.

The export is also designed to remain useful when an individual file cannot be recovered. LogGPT Plus saves the conversation and every artifact it successfully downloads, then records failures or skipped items in the manifest rather than discarding the entire archive.

This ZIP is a durable capture package. It can be retained as-is or processed offline with tools such as Extract Chat to produce Markdown or HTML that refers to the preserved local files.

LogGPT has no user accounts, analytics, telemetry, or purchase server, and it does not send your conversation to an external processing service. LogGPT Plus is a permanent in-app purchase available through the existing Mac App Store listing.
