==================================================
LINKEDIN
==================================================

ChatGPT moved its conversation controls again. LogGPT 1.3.1 brought the download button back, but while switching conversations we found a race in the follow-up button handling that could freeze Safari.

LogGPT 1.3.2 fixes that race. The extension now skips hidden conversation headers, avoids trying to insert the button relative to itself, and handles page changes without repeatedly rebuilding the button. You should be able to move between conversations and still find Download where you expect it, without reloading the page.

This is a stability fix; the export workflow itself has not changed. I've submitted 1.3.2 to Apple, and existing owners will receive the update at no additional charge when it becomes available.

Read the update: https://unixwzrd.ai/s/c6c0f02119/
