LINKEDIN
==================================================

LogGPT is adding another conversation source: Dots, alongside ChatGPT and ChatGPT Work.

Version 1.3.4 exports the main Dots timeline as JSON. The download button sits beside the Dots conversation actions menu, and longer histories show collection progress with cancellation available.

Want the files as well as the conversation? The optional LogGPT Plus upgrade keeps supported attachments with the conversation in one portable ZIP. It's a one-time in-app purchase for Basic owners who want artifact archiving. Existing Plus owners are already covered, including the new Dots attachments.

The scope is specific: main timeline and direct available attachments. Linked task histories and nested reply histories are not included.

Recognized sources also get clearer filename postfixes: -chat, -work, or -dot. If the source cannot be determined, the filename keeps the chat name and start/end dates without a source postfix. A small change, but useful when your Downloads folder has become a conversation archive.

LogGPT 1.3.4 is available on the Mac App Store. The app update is included at no additional charge for all existing LogGPT owners, Basic and Plus alike.

Title:
LogGPT 1.3.4 adds Dots timeline exports and source-labeled filenames - now on the Mac App Store

Body:
Disclosure: I develop LogGPT, a Safari extension for exporting conversations and available attachments.

Version 1.3.4 is now on the Mac App Store. It adds Dots as an export source alongside ChatGPT and ChatGPT Work. The LogGPT button appears beside the Dots conversation actions menu. Basic exports the main timeline as JSON; Plus can package it with directly attached available files in a ZIP. Longer histories show message collection progress, and retrieval can be cancelled.

The important limit: this covers the main timeline and its direct available attachments, not linked task histories or nested reply histories. It is not a recursive export of everything reachable from a Dots conversation.

When the source is recognized, filenames also get -chat, -work, or -dot postfixes. Otherwise, the filename keeps the chat name with its start and end dates, without a source postfix. Version 1.3.4 retains the checkbox visibility fixes from 1.3.3.

All existing LogGPT owners, Basic or Plus, get the app update at no additional charge. If you want the supporting files alongside your conversations, Plus is an optional one-time in-app purchase. Existing Plus owners don't need to buy it again for Dots archiving.
