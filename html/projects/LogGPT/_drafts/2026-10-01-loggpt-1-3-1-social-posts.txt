ChatGPT changed its conversation title bar, and LogGPT's download button stopped showing up where it should. LogGPT 1.3.1 fixes that.

The button now appears just before Share in the current ChatGPT layout. The extension also handles earlier layouts and puts the button back if ChatGPT replaces the title bar as you move between conversations.

The export workflow hasn't changed; this update makes sure you can get to it. If you already own LogGPT, the App Store will deliver 1.3.1 at no additional charge when Apple makes it available.
