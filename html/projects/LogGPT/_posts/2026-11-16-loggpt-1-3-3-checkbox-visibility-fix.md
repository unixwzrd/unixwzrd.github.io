ChatGPT's web interface changed again, and its page styles were interfering with LogGPT's own controls. **LogGPT 1.3.3 fixes missing checkboxes in the LogGPT Plus export dialog and Basic upgrade notice.** The update is currently in Apple's App Store review.

<!--more-->

The Plus export dialog lets you choose generated content, uploaded content, or both for your archive. Those choices need to be visible, not just present somewhere on the page. Version 1.3.3 improves compatibility with ChatGPT's page styles so the download options remain visible and usable, including the affected checkboxes in the Basic upgrade notice.

LogGPT for Safari exports ChatGPT conversations as structured JSON. The optional LogGPT Plus upgrade adds available generated and uploaded files, packaged with the conversation and an artifact manifest in a portable ZIP. If you are new to the extension, the [LogGPT project overview](/projects/LogGPT/) explains the two options and how to get started.

**Version 1.3.3 is in review, not yet generally available.** Existing owners will receive the update through the App Store at no additional charge once Apple approves and releases it.

[View LogGPT on the Mac App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/loggpt/id6743342693?mt=12).
