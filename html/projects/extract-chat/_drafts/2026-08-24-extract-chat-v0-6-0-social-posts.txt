LINKEDIN
==================================================

A ChatGPT conversation is often more than text.

It may include generated images, uploaded documents, spreadsheets, audio, source archives, and other files that gave the discussion its context. LogGPT Plus can preserve those materials with the conversation in a ZIP. extract-chat v0.6.0 can now turn that archive into a readable, reusable local record.

The release adds:

- safe ingestion of LogGPT Plus ZIP archives, including traversal and symlink defenses
- local organization and relinking of generated, uploaded, and derived artifacts
- combined Markdown and HTML export
- canonical output names that keep each conversation and its files together
- turn-aware continuity chunks with overlap and a hard 524,288-byte limit
- an initial native macOS front end for selecting JSON or ZIP input and common export options

Original artifacts remain available. Small CSV or TSV files may be shown as readable tables, but the source downloads are not replaced by those previews.

The division of responsibility is straightforward: LogGPT Plus captures the authenticated conversation and artifacts; extract-chat processes the resulting package locally. There is no hosted conversion service in the middle.

Title:
extract-chat v0.6.0: From LogGPT Plus ZIP to a Usable Local Archive

Subtitle:
Safely unpack a preserved ChatGPT conversation, keep its artifacts, and turn the result into readable Markdown, HTML, and continuity chunks.

Body:

A useful ChatGPT conversation is rarely just text. It may include generated images, uploaded documents, spreadsheets, audio, source archives, and other files that supplied important context.

LogGPT Plus can preserve those materials with the conversation in a ZIP archive. extract-chat v0.6.0 can now consume that archive and turn it into a readable, portable local record.

The original JSON workflow remains available, but the same positional input now accepts a LogGPT Plus ZIP. extract-chat finds the conversation, organizes generated, uploaded, and derived artifacts under a conversation-named output directory, and creates Markdown, HTML, or both. Links in those documents prefer the local preserved files.

Archive handling is defensive. Version 0.6.0 rejects absolute paths, traversal attempts, and symbolic-link members before extraction. A local archive should be able to populate its assigned export directory, not choose arbitrary destinations elsewhere on the machine.

The release also preserves a useful distinction between an artifact and its presentation. Small CSV or TSV files may be displayed as tables, but their original downloads remain available. A readable preview does not replace the source file.

For long conversations, extract-chat can create continuity chunks at turn boundaries with optional overlap. Final Markdown parts are measured after their headers and overlap are applied, and each part is held to a hard 524,288-byte maximum. This produces more deliberate handoff material than cutting a transcript at arbitrary byte positions.

An initial native macOS front end is also included for selecting JSON or ZIP input and common export controls. The CLI remains the primary public interface, and the macOS application still requires a separately built and signed helper before App Store distribution.

The workflow between the two projects is now clear: LogGPT Plus captures the authenticated conversation and its artifacts; the ZIP becomes a durable local handoff; and extract-chat validates and renders that package. No hosted conversion service is required, and the original archive remains available for later reprocessing.
