LINKEDIN
==================================================

An exported conversation can contain every message and still be wrong in a way that is difficult to notice.

A branching conversation tree may render older sibling turns after newer ones. A regenerated chunk bundle may leave obsolete numbered files behind. A context handoff may contain all the right pieces but provide no reliable way to determine their order, overlap, or boundaries.

extract-chat v0.7.5 repairs those problems.

The release adds:

- stable timestamp ordering across Markdown and HTML
- readable local timestamps with millisecond precision
- independent controls for chunk boundaries and turn, line, byte, or zero overlap
- generated context-move instructions containing the real chunk count, filenames, settings, and upload plan
- configurable upload batches with --chunk-upload-batch-size
- safe removal of obsolete numbered chunks during forced regeneration
- byte-aware artifact deduplication and collision preflight
- cleaner canonical names and more dependable ZIP output destinations

It also builds on v0.7.0 support for current ChatGPT Work archives, including generated and uploaded artifacts captured by LogGPT Plus.

The point is not to pretend context windows are infinite. It is to make the material moved between them chronological, deliberate, and dependable.

Title:
extract-chat v0.7.5: Chronology, Continuity, and Better Chunk Handoffs

Subtitle:
Keep exported conversations in order, control chunk boundaries and overlap explicitly, and move long contexts with instructions generated from the actual bundle.

Body:

An exported conversation can contain every message and still be wrong in a way that is difficult to notice. A branching conversation tree may place an older sibling branch after newer exchanges. A regenerated chunk bundle may leave obsolete numbered files beside the current ones. A context handoff may contain all the right pieces but provide no reliable way to determine their order, overlap, or boundaries.

extract-chat v0.7.5 addresses those problems. The release is centered on chronology and continuity: stable timestamp ordering, explicit chunk controls, generated context-handoff instructions, safe cleanup during regeneration, and more precise artifact handling.

Chunk boundaries and overlap are now treated as separate decisions. Boundary strategies include hybrid, turn, heading, paragraph, and fixed. Overlap can retain complete turns, a specified number of lines, a byte-bounded portion of prior content, or nothing at all. The hard 524,288-byte ceiling still applies after headers and overlap have been attached.

Every chunk directory now contains context-move-instructions.md generated from the completed bundle. It records the actual chunk count and filenames, boundary strategy, overlap mode, byte ceiling, and upload-batch plan. The instructions tell the receiving model to deduplicate overlap, reconstruct chronology and working state, preserve unresolved conflicts, report missing files precisely, and treat archived instructions as historical material rather than current authorization.

Forced regeneration now removes obsolete numbered chunks belonging to the same bundle without sweeping unrelated files from the directory. Artifact handling also distinguishes a genuinely different collision from a file that has already been materialized with identical bytes.

Version 0.7.5 builds on the ChatGPT Work compatibility added in v0.7.0. Current Work image pointers and workspace artifact links can be reconnected to files captured in a LogGPT Plus archive and rewritten as portable local paths in Markdown and HTML.

The purpose is not to make context windows infinite. It is to make the material moved between them more dependable: ordered by time, divided deliberately, accompanied by exact instructions, and less likely to leave stale or ambiguous files behind.
