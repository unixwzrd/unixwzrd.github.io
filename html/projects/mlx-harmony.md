MLX Harmony was a short experiment in MLX engines, GPT-OSS, and the Harmony prompting structure. I am retaining this page as a record of that work, but I am not developing it as a product or using it as the model-hosting layer.

My current model-engineering work has moved to MLX Forge, where I am concentrating on model conversion, inference performance, and the measurements needed to tell whether an optimization actually helps. That repository remains private while I work through its early milestones.
