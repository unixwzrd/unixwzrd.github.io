Not every hidden mark is a watermark.

Not every watermark proves authorship.

Not every provenance record should be stripped.

UnicodeFix v2.0.0—the Ghostmark Edition—is built around those distinctions.

This release turns UnicodeFix into an evidence-based local audit and safe-cleanup tool. It can inventory hidden Unicode and security signals, recognize local C2PA provenance carriers, audit Markdown wrapping, inspect supported payloads in source comments, and run explicitly configured local watermark or authorship profiles.

The important part is what it does not claim.

A curly quote is typography, not proof that a chatbot wrote the text. Column-80 wrapping is formatting. C2PA records provenance. A statistical result applies only to the named detector, configuration, artifacts, and threshold used to produce it.

Ghostmark separates findings into provenance, Unicode security, known-watermark, authorship-signal, typography, and formatting categories. Reports retain locations, confidence, removability, and planned actions rather than collapsing unlike evidence into one mystery score.

The workflow is deliberate:

- audit without changing the file
- preview the exact transformation and diff
- clean only supported findings
- preserve recognized provenance unless removal is explicit
- keep watermark and authorship claims attached to their detector and limitations

Everything runs locally after installation. Your text is not uploaded to a detector, vendor, model service, or external manifest server.

Subtitle:

Body:

Not every hidden mark is a watermark.

Not every watermark proves authorship.

Not every provenance record should be stripped.

UnicodeFix v2.0.0—the Ghostmark Edition—is built around those distinctions.

The original UnicodeFix solved a direct, practical problem: invisible characters and unusual typography can make text look normal while breaking parsers, diffs, configuration files, documentation, or source code.

Version 2.0 keeps that practical cleaner but adds a much more careful evidence model. It can audit hidden Unicode and security signals, recognize local C2PA provenance carriers, identify formatting such as hard-wrapped Markdown, inspect supported hidden payloads in source comments, and run explicitly configured local watermark or authorship profiles.

Those findings are not collapsed into one generic AI score. Ghostmark separates provenance, Unicode security, known-watermark results, authorship signals, typography, and formatting. Detailed reports retain the location, confidence, removability, planned action, and applicable detector or limitation.

That separation matters. A curly quote is typography. Column-80 wrapping is formatting. C2PA is provenance. A statistical watermark result applies only to the named detector, configuration, artifacts, key or seed, and threshold used to produce it. An authorship signal is probabilistic evidence under a pinned and calibrated reference model, not proof of who wrote a paragraph.

The new workflow makes the intended level of intervention explicit. Report mode audits without changing a file. Dry-run and diff modes preview the exact requested transformation. Cleanup removes supported findings, while recognized provenance remains protected unless `--strip-provenance` is explicitly requested.

Markdown and source cleanup have their own guardrails. Markdown unwrapping is opt-in and preserves meaningful document structure. Source mode distinguishes comments from strings, identifiers, and syntax, cleans only supported comment payloads, and verifies parsing before and after the transformation.

The optional watermark and authorship lab also stays local. It requires explicitly configured profiles and matching artifacts already on the machine. UnicodeFix does not download a mystery detector at runtime, contact a vendor service, or claim it can find every unknown or proprietary scheme.

Everything runs locally after installation. Your text is not uploaded to a detector, model service, or external manifest server.
